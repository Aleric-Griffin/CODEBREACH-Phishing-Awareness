# CODEBREACH - Public Awareness

## What is this campaign?

CODEBREACH is a cybersecurity campaign that targets the general public and helps teach and explain how to protect your digital self-online and your information.

## Why this campaign?

CODEBREACH helps people to be able to easily recognise common cyber threats and be able to prevent their accounts from being taken over.

## What this project focuses on

Phishing, Malware and Social Engineering.
