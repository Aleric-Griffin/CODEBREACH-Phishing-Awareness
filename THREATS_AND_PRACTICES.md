THREAT	WHAT IT IS	HOW TO PROTECT YOURSELF

   PHISHING	WHAT IS THIS?
This is a form of social engineering where criminals send victims links though email designed to trick them into revealing sensitive information or installing malware into their device.	HOW TO PREVENT/AVOID
check and verify the emails you receive that require you giving important information by checking any spelling errors or if the supposed organization’s name is spelled correctly with the right letters in the email. Always have multi-factor authentication enabled on your accounts and if possible verify with customer support why you got the email or message.

   MALWARE	WHAT IS IT?
These are harmful apps, attachments or downloads that can spy, damage files or lock a device.	HOW TO PREVENT/AVOID
avoid downloading apps from unknown or untrusted sites and always have a up to date antivirus software on your device if possible.

   SOCIAL ENGINEERING	WHAT IT IS?
This is when someone poses as a figure of a certain position like a manager, supplier or IT worker and ask for access to information.	HOW TO PREVENT/AVOID
Always verify the identity of anyone requesting important information and always be wary with unexpected requests from any supposed important figure.
